## First-sale position

- **Shared-database isolation is a prerequisite for the first paying clinic**, not work to defer until a second clinic. Selected-clinic authorization, browser/session boundaries, public tokens, files, search and background operations must all agree on the tenant.
- Preserve the existing modular application, PostgreSQL, notification interface, Typesense fallback and CI. Repair their boundaries and failure behavior rather than replace them with new infrastructure.
- Part 1 clinical and money safeguards remain prerequisites. A financial report cannot reconcile incorrect payment postings; a durable outbox cannot make an invalid sync payload safe.
- Invitation acceptance and actual invitation/password-reset email delivery are core onboarding/recovery gates. Registered SMTP and a generated link are not proof of delivery or usable clinic membership.
- Clinical drafts belong to P2-02 privacy/server save-and-load parity, not the FR-22 outbox. FR-22 remains registration/billing only; no general clinical offline cache is proposed.
- Patient collections go directly to the clinic. Optional Razorpay remote links/subscriptions and device/mobile offerings need acceptance only when included in the sale. Pharmacy can be excluded only when disabled and contractually excluded.
- Checkmarks follow the ledger's **I/F** dispositions only; **P/X/D** items remain unchecked. Proposed owners are roles, not assigned signatories; named accountable owners, supporting reviewers and final release approvers still need recorded acceptance.

### 2.3 Data, identity, money and public-token paths

**Patient data.** Registration and purpose/guardian consent lead to appointment/precheck, arrival/queue, triage and signed consultation versions. Lab orders/results feed review; amendments preserve originals. Billing and confirmed receipts feed reports. Pharmacy fulfillment and counter sales have their own traceable stock/money effects. Do not silently rewrite clinical originals, consent, accounting or audit history to correct defects.

**Selected identity.** `Tenant.Id` → `Clinic.TenantId` is the ownership relationship; `Clinic.Id` remains a clinic identifier. A multi-membership user may select clinic B while their global/primary `User.ClinicId` still points to A. Use validated selected tenant/clinic and current active membership-role claims, not that global field or browser-supplied role/tenant. P2-03 covers OIDC issuance, code exchange, refresh and Userinfo as well as business handlers: copied old claims do not prove current membership or credential/session validity. Platform access must be explicit, especially outside HTTP.

**Active authentication flows.** `Program.cs` enables OpenIddict authorization-code flow with required PKCE and refresh-token flow; it does **not** enable the password grant. The `IsPasswordGrantType()` branch in `AuthorizationController` is present but is not an active supported grant. Keep `grant_type=password` disabled and test protocol denial. The separate `AuthController` password login/cookie-refresh workflow remains distinct and needs its own selected-membership/session tests.

**Onboarding and recovery.** `PlatformAdminController.ProvisionTenant` and `StaffController.Invite` now share `HashToken()` with `StaffController.AcceptInvite`, which creates the intended active membership. Provisioning/staff/reset callers use the existing email sender. Provisioning atomically retains a DP-protected response receipt and exact request fingerprint for authorized original-token/expiry/outcome replay; migrated-controller and intercepted UI proof now exist. A token hash alone cannot recreate a lost plaintext link. Durable DP recovery, complete HTTP onboarding, approved affected-invite reissuance and real mailbox receipt remain G08/G09; transport acceptance is not mailbox receipt.

**Money.** Funds go from the patient to the clinic's cash/bank/merchant account. A PSP event, signed device event or authorized manual receipt is evidence consumed by the software ledger, not money held by SAMSTACK. All accepted receipts must use consistent invoice balance/status, confirmed payment and balanced posting rules. Provider IDs support real refunds; an invented ID cannot prove payment/refund. The optional reconciliation worker must remain disabled until captured-only polling has verified parity with the finalized Part 1 accounting path; tenant scoping alone is not that proof. SaaS subscription billing is separate from patient collections.

**Public links.** Queue tracking, precheck submission and prescription verification use narrowly authorized tokens. An opaque token must resolve only its own resource/tenant before protected joins occur. `IgnoreQueryFilters()` bypasses EF filtering, **not PostgreSQL RLS**. Never solve an anonymous join failure with blanket anonymous PHI access or an unrestricted platform-admin database context. A signed webhook instead requires provider authentication/signature plus authoritative tenant routing and replay controls.
